# Known issues: R4-P2

## Infantry rendering
Infantry are visible in Basilisk II but invisible on the physical Macintosh IIci, even after R4-P2 redraw changes.

## Carryall rendering
The Harvester appears after delivery, but the Carryall delivery animation is missing on physical hardware.

## Movement smoothness
R4-P1 improved unit responsiveness on physical hardware but movement became visually choppy. R4-P2 retains that behavior.

## Performance
Prioritize reducing total rendering and simulation workload. Evaluate responsiveness and scrolling on real IIci hardware, not just in Basilisk II.
